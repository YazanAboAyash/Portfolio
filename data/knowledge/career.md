# Yazan's career

## Experience in short

Yazan has been coding for about 5 years, and has worked professionally as a developer for about 1.5 years. He is an IHK-certified Fachinformatiker for application development. His certifications include Python (PCEP), EU AI Act training, GitHub Foundations and GitHub Copilot, and the IHK Fachinformatiker qualification.

## Current jobs

Yazan works full-time, split part-time between two companies: avarno GmbH (since March 2025) and Botgenossen GmbH (since February 2026). At both he works as a full-stack developer and AI engineer.

## What he builds at work

At avarno and Botgenossen, Yazan builds AI-driven solutions: RAG systems, automations, chatbots, voice bots and live avatars. The main stack is TypeScript, Node.js, Next.js and Python, with PostgreSQL and pgvector, hosting on AWS and Hetzner, and Microsoft Azure: Azure AI, the Azure portal, Microsoft Entra ID and Azure hosting. GDPR (DSGVO) and the EU AI Act are part of every project.

## Proudest work

The project Yazan is proudest of is a live AI avatar of the mayor of Büttelborn, a town in Hesse, Germany.

## How he got into development

Yazan started on his own because he liked building websites and web apps, and taught himself before doing his IHK retraining (Umschulung) as a Fachinformatiker for application development. He did the practical training at avarno GmbH, where he works today.

## Community and open source

Yazan contributes to open-source projects. He also runs daily sessions with the interns at work.
